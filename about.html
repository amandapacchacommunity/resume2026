About

Analytical thinking with a creative foundation.

“I help leaders understand complex organizations well enough to make thoughtful, ethical, evidence-based decisions.”

I have built my career around the places where functions overlap: where policy meets operations, numbers need context, and leaders need a clear path through ambiguity.

My approach combines data integrity, active listening, visual communication, and respect for the people affected by organizational decisions. I see risk management as a way to create the clarity and confidence needed for thoughtful innovation.

An art-school education shaped how I observe, question, and synthesize. Experience in higher education, museums, nonprofit advancement, and public administration taught me how to apply those instincts inside complex institutions.
